# GTM

## Exact Target User

**Primary:** Engineering Manager or CTO at a Series A startup (5-30 engineers), where the team has adopted AI coding tools in the last 18 months but nobody has audited the stack. Company is spending $2k-$15k/month on AI tools spread across Cursor, Copilot, Claude, and ChatGPT — often on overlapping plans because different team members onboarded different tools independently.

**Secondary:** Bootstrapped founder with a 2-5 person technical team who got sticker shock on their AI tool bills and has no benchmark.

**Not the target:** Enterprise (10k+ employees have procurement teams), solo developers (low-value leads for Credex), non-technical founders (won't be able to evaluate the audit).

## First 100 Users in 30 Days ($0 Budget)

**Week 1 (Days 1-7):**
- Post on Hacker News: "Show HN: I built a free AI tool spend auditor for startups" — target 50 signups from this alone
- Share in Rands Leadership Slack #tools channel with a genuine post: "Built this after auditing our own stack — found $400/mo we were wasting. Sharing in case useful."
- DM 20 CTOs/EMs on X who have recently tweeted about AI tool costs or Cursor/Copilot

**Week 2 (Days 8-14):**
- Post in r/ExperiencedDevs with a real data post: "Audited 50 startup AI stacks — here's what I found" (use early audit data)
- Reach out to The Pragmatic Engineer, Bytes newsletter, TLDR Tech for a mention (cold email with the tool as the hook)
- Post 3 tweets: one with a specific counterintuitive finding ("ChatGPT Team is almost always wrong for <5 person teams — here's why")

**Week 3-4 (Days 15-30):**
- Ask first 50 users to share their audit URL on X/LinkedIn — the shareable URL is designed for this
- Publish a data post on Substack: "The AI Tool Spend Report: what 100 startup audits revealed"
- Submit to Product Hunt on Day 21 (by then: testimonials, real data, polished)

## The Unfair Distribution Channel

Credex already has relationships with startups that purchased AI credits. Email those customers: "Here's a free tool to audit if you're getting full value from your AI stack." This is warm outreach to people who have already signaled they care about AI cost efficiency. No cold traffic needed for the first 200 users.
