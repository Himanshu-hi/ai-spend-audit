## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free? What's the catch?**
A: Yes, fully free. SpendSight is a tool built by Credex — we sell discounted AI credits for teams with real AI spend. The audit is how we find people we can actually help. If your audit shows <$100/month in savings, we'll just say so. We don't manufacture savings to create a sales opportunity.

**Q: Do I need to create an account?**
A: No. Fill in your tools, get your audit instantly. Email is optional — only requested after you've seen your results, never before.

**Q: How accurate is the pricing data?**
A: Every number in the audit traces to a vendor's official pricing page, with the date it was verified. See PRICING_DATA.md for sources. Pricing changes; we update monthly.

**Q: What happens to my data?**
A: Your audit inputs (tools, spend, team size) are stored to generate your shareable report URL. Email addresses are stored separately and never shared with third parties. Shared audit URLs strip identifying information (email, company name) from the public view.

**Q: What if my AI tool isn't listed?**
A: We support the 8 most common AI tools used by startups. If you use something not listed, skip it — the audit is still useful for the tools we do cover. We're adding more tools based on user requests.
