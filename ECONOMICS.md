# ECONOMICS

## What a Converted Lead Is Worth to Credex

Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits. Assuming:
- Average first purchase: $2,000 in credits (conservative — startups buying Cursor/Claude Enterprise at discount)
- Gross margin on credits: ~20% (discount arbitrage business, thin margins)
- Gross profit per sale: **$400**
- Repeat purchase rate: 60% (companies that need credits once, need them again in 3-6 months)
- LTV over 18 months (3 purchases avg): $400 × 3 = **$1,200 LTV per customer**

## Conversion Funnel

```
100 visitors
  → 40 complete audit (40% — form is short, value is immediate)
  → 8 submit email (20% of completers — shown after value)
  → 2 book Credex consult (25% of high-savings cases)
  → 1 purchase credits (50% close rate on consultation)
```

Break-even math:
- Cost to run the tool: ~$0.03/audit (Anthropic API + Supabase + Resend ≈ $3 per 100 audits)
- Revenue per 100 visitors: 1 customer × $400 gross profit = **$400**
- **ROI: ~13,000%** — this is why lead-gen content products work

## What Conversion Rate Makes This Profitable

At $0 CAC (organic), literally any sale is profitable. The tool needs to:
1. Drive 100 audits → generates ~1 Credex customer
2. Cost < $400 to operate for those 100 audits

At current cost ($3/100 audits): ✅ Already profitable at 1% audit→customer rate.

The real question is audit volume. At 1,000 audits/month:
- 10 Credex customers/month
- $4,000 gross profit/month from the tool's leads alone
- Annual from tool: **$48,000 GP**

This doesn't count brand value, SEO, and word-of-mouth from shared audit URLs.

## Path to $1M ARR in 18 Months

$1M ARR ÷ $2,000 avg order = **500 customers/year = ~42/month**

Month 1-3: 50 audits/month → 2-3 customers/month (organic only)
Month 4-6: HN post + newsletter mention → 500 audits/month → 15-20 customers/month
Month 7-12: Data flywheel (published audit reports attract SEO) → 2,000 audits/month → 40-50 customers/month
Month 13-18: Referral loop + enterprise outreach → 5,000 audits/month → 100+ customers/month

**The critical assumption:** Audit→consultation conversion stays at 25% for high-savings cases. This holds if:
- The audit recommendations are accurate (credible, defensible)
- The Credex consultation provides demonstrably better savings than the audit alone surfaced
- High-savings users ($500+/mo) are the ones most motivated to act

**What would break this:** If audit quality is low (bad recommendations), users won't trust Credex. The audit engine's defensibility is the moat.
